Originally from the UK, Pete planted himself in the Canadian scene at a young age, Organizing and DJing events led to a few of Calgary’s long running club residencies. He made the natural move to production in early 2000s and with more than 20 years experience behind him, continues to tour the North American festival circuit during summer and studio work during the long Canadian winters making music under his name as well as his 2 alter egos 'Bungalow' & 'Chop Xtra'.
  Most recently he founded a record label in 2021, Wildfire Recordings, that boasts an international cast of producers and a monthly radio show on UK based Cutter's Choice Radio.
In 2023 Pete won YYCMA (Calgary Music Awards) “EDM Recording of the Year’ for his track ‘Just Dance’ by alter-ego Bungalow.
