Mystic State are a UK-based electronic music production duo who have carved out a distinct space in the scene with their complex, soulful, and cinematic productions. Blending influences from drum and bass, dub, and ambient sound design, their productions balance low-end pressure with intricate percussion and rich atmospheres, creating a sound which is uniquely theirs.

Emerging from Bristol’s underground circuit, Mystic State have built a
reputation for supporting local community by collaborating with fellow peers and supporting local music in their DJ sets across the globe. The music within their DJ sets is often comprised of releases from the pair’s respective record labels - The Moth Club & The Chikara Project, two imprints which the duo frequently release on themselves.

2026 has seen the duo firing out releases on labels White Peach, Flexout Audio and YUKU. Their third album, “Threads”, is due to drop on the Chikara Project in the early summer, a release which will see the duo explore different tempos and moods. Will & Mike are constantly showcasing a commitment to quality and originality, and continue to push the boundaries of electronic music while maintaining a timeless and unmistakable identity.
