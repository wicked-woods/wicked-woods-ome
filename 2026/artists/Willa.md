Willa
Glitchhop / Dubstep / Drum&bass

    For 20 years, Willa has been tearing through dancefloors with uncompromising basslines and high energy selections built to move bodies and shake soundsystems. A self proclaimed bassline skid from cranbrook B.C, Willa brings raw kootenay grit to every stage ‐ blending Glitchhop, Dubstep and Drum&Bass into a relentless, peak time assault.

    This isn't background music, This is full-throttle, chest pounding，sub heavy, glitch chaos engineered for the party.

    Known for massive bass lines, precision mixing and explosive drops, Willa crafts his sets that build tension and detonate without warning. Every set/performance is designed with one goal in mind: guaranteed party rocker status.

    With appearances at heavyweight festivals like Shambhala and Wicked Woods, Willa has proven the ability to command both underground crowds and large scale festival stages alike.

    From late night forest sessions to sweat soaked club dance floors, Willa delivers 2 decades of experience, heavy artillery selections, and unstoppable energy- pushing bass culture forward one drop at a time.

    After a two year hiatus from attending festivals, Willa returns recharged, refocused and more ready than ever. Now back behind the decks, the energy is raw, and the tracks hit harder than ever and the mission is clear: reclaim dancefloors and remind crowds exactly why the party never does.
