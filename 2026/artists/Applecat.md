AppleCat is an internationally acclaimed DJ, producer, and vocalist from Canada’s West Coast, known for her blend of Deep Dubstep, Fairytale Bass, Trap, and various world influences into cinematic, grimy performances. Her sets feel less like a DJ set and more like a spell: bass-driven, emotional, and rooted in the belief that “a revolution without dancing is not a revolution worth having.”

Onstage, she fuses live mashups, reworks, and ethereal vocals into hypnotic basslines that move audiences both physically and emotionally. This dark booty bouncing dubwitch bends the perception of genre exemplifying her unparalleled je ne sais quoi.”

She has performed across North America, the UK, and beyond, including Burning Man, Enchanted Forest Gathering, Rifflandia, Future Forest, Evolve, Envision, and Tall Tree, sharing lineups with CloZee, Truth, Emancipator, Tipper, The Polish Ambassador, and The Halluci Nation.

Featured in the global documentary Amplify Her (2017) and interviewed in DJ Mag, AppleCat continues to evolve as a primal storyteller and sonic architect.

Music is a possession that I desire no exorcism from.  - AppleCat
