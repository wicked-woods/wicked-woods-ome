Lainey Kate, (previously known at KVTE) has been a DJ for seven years and has recently made a comeback in the Calgary scene after taking a 1.5 year long hiatus from playing shows. You can find her playing spacey/groovy tech house and indie dance, emotional melodic/deep house, and also high energy peak-time techno sets in the Calgary area. Her sets are influenced by the sound of acid, throughout the genres she plays. In 2021, she made her festival debuts in Calgary, at Badlands Music Festival and Chasing Summer Family Reunion. In 2025, she made her festival comeback with a MainStage set at Chasing Summer. Opening up for artists such as Dillon Francis, Cosmic Gate, Marie Vaunt and J.Worra; her shows have taken her all the way to Texas where she has played events in Austin, and Dallas alongside the likes of Talldoor Records and a festival in San Marcos with Apogee Presents: Martian Arts Festival. Lainey Kate is currently affiliated and can be seen playing shows in Calgary with Beehive Presents, Blueprint, Live Nation, Cynikil Entertainment, Technu, Pop-Up Series, and After Hrs Club.
