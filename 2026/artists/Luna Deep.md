Luna Deep is a Central Alberta-based DJ rooted in deep basslines, rolling rhythms, and atmospheric textures. With over a decade behind the decks and a background in performance art and dance, her sets are immersive, intentional, and felt as much as they are heard.

While she’s known to play a little something for everyone - depending on the phase of Luna - her true resonance lives in drum & bass. Blending liquid, dark, and melodic elements, she guides the floor through tension and release, building energy before pulling it deep.

known for performances at Paradise Music Festival, The Womp, and Wicked Woods, Luna brings a raw yet refined presence to every stage.

Her sound is for the ones who really feel it - hypnotic, high-vibrational, and unapologetically deep.
