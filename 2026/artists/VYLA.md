Vyla is an emerging dubstep artist from Calgary, AB, crafting deep, heavy basslines that nod to the roots of the genre while pulling inspiration from techno, and hypnotic groove. Her sound is about pushing boundaries, and designed for large sound systems. Since starting her project just over a year ago, she has played for crews like Subshakra, Infinite Sounds, Sacred Roots, Bass Friends Hub, Obscura, and Dark Matter Threads.
appearances at festivals such as Deeproots, Neon Jungle, Bass Summit, Moonlight, Unalone and Restart Music Festival. With mixes on radio shows like Coco Dubz’s Subtle Radio show, What Would The Neighbors Think, and Sub.Fm. Her sound has spread globally. As a growing presence in the underground scene, she has released with Dub Den Records, Dub Hustle Records, and forthcoming releases with Incurzion Audio & Sateyo Sounds.
