Holden Space 

JEFF HOLDEN sync’s Ableton Live with the RC 505 loop station creating a unique blend of original songs intersperse with improv and rapid-fire freestyles. Jeff is a producer, word worker, multi-instrumentalist and a music therapist inspired by Hip Hop, Trap, World beat grooves and the rhythms of nature. From the Shambhala Music Festival stage to the commodore Ball Room to individual sound healing and small community circles, Holden Space brings good energy to the people.
