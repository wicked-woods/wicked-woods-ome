Having only moved to Bristol in September, Anais is a prime example of dedication to the art of DJing. With numerous sets under her belt across Bristol and beyond coupled with a hard-hitting mixing style, Anais is certainly one to watch moving forward. She specialises in mixing DnB, however also a bit of a wizard at mixing tek and a bit of garage.
