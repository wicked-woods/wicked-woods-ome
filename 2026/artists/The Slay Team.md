The Slay Team—The FloWarrior, The Flying Spark, The Flowbot, and Amanda Panda—are multidisciplinary performance artists with backgrounds in dance, flow, circus, and martial arts. 
They are renowned within the festival community as masters of their craft, each blending decades of training with their own unique style, presence, and unforgettable stage presence.
These artists don’t just perform—they command space, captivate attention, and turn moments into memories, leaving audiences energized, inspired, and wanting more.
