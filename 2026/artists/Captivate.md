Captivate is a producer and DJ based on the ancestral lands of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples. His sound drifts through the UK rave, Jamaican dub, and ambient continuums, often refracted through the lens of leftfield techno. His DJ sets dissolve genre boundaries, prioritizing pressure, narrative, and rhythmic exploration. He has performed at festivals including Bass Coast and Shambhala Music Festival, and his music has received longstanding support from influences including Om Unit, Doctor Jeep, and Mia Koden.
