Johnny Chan commonly known as (Blue Dragon) is a World Bass musician and storyteller in sound, blending Asian-inspired instrumentation with deep electronic bass music to create cinematic, mystical, and emotionally immersive experiences. Well known for the Two Rivers release on  High Vibe Records, Blue Dragon incorporates guzheng, flute, erhu, atmospheric textures, and spiritual themes into modern EDM, bridging Eastern culture with contemporary electronic music. Inspired by nature, mythology, and human connection, the Blue Dragon track made its breakout during EDC Thailand, where lines of tuk tuks traveling to and from the festival were seen playing Blue Dragon music throughout the streets of Thailand. 

About me: 
Johnny Chan is a World Bass artist, Chinese Trained producer, and storyteller in sound from the Spokane Tribe of Indians reservation whose life has been shaped by international business, music, travel, and cultural exchange. Performing under the name Blue Dragon, he blends Asian-inspired instrumentation with deep electronic bass music to create cinematic and emotionally immersive experiences inspired by mythology, spirituality, nature, and human connection. Johnny Chan gained international attention during EDC Thailand when tuk tuks lined throughout the streets to and from the festival were seen playing Blue Dragon music, helping introduce his sound to a global audience.
