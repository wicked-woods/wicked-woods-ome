Originating from the mountains of the West Kootenays, British Columbia, Neuronic has been holding it down on the decks for the past 2 decades, pushing the sounds of underground electronic music. With major influence from peers in the Canadian underground scene as well as taking inspiration from artists all around the globe, especially The UK and German scenes, the 34 year Canadian DJ and producer brings forth a very diverse and eclectic combination of music to the dance floor. His introduction to rave music and culture began in the early 2000s, participating in local legendary bush raves such as Onefest and Loki Festival, thanks to friends like Grandpa Phunk, CBiggs, Ion Eye, Application and many more!

As the the same suggests, you can expect a broad range of experimental glitched out sound design that will tickle your neurons and have your synapses firing in ways you could never Imagine! Bringing a variety of Bass Music, Techno, Deep Heady Grooves and Global Rhythms to mix, Neuronic guarantees to take the listener on a journey, inspire new forms of movement and utilize the given space to its full potential!
