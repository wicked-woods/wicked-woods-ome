Hanna SoLow is a multifaceted DJ and event curator from Edmonton, Alberta, who tells a story through her dynamic sets. An aficionado of beats that make the bass bins go “brrrrrrr”, bass music is her bread and butter. From deep dubstep, to UKG, to baile, to DNB, her sets will keep you guessing and are guaranteed to ignite the dancefloor. 

A longtime contributor to the Canadian music scene, Hanna SoLow has poured her heart into events and festivals throughout Western Canada. As a DJ, Hanna SoLow has performed alongside Truth, The Librarian, Youngsta, Moody Good, Super Future and ONHELL among others, and has performed at festivals such as Wicked Woods, The Womp and Downtown Defrost. This year, she has started to learn how to produce music and is looking forward to continuing to learn, grow and push forward in her musical journey.
