Named by BBC Radio as “One of the most brilliant world-fusion producers of our time”, David Starfire is known as the
pioneer of the Global Bass genre and composer of music for meditation and psychedelic assisted therapy. Billboard
summed it up best, “David Starfire brings a unique perspective to EDM.”
David has performed at prominent festivals such as Winter Music Conference, Coachella, Outside Lands, Envision,
Burning Man, Eclipse, and Lightning in a Bottle. He’s been direct support for the likes of Skrillex, CloZee, and collaborated
with top artists such as Afrika Bambaataa, Sizzla, Alex Grey, Ram Dass, Alan Watts, Ooah (The Glitch Mob), Sidecar
Tommy (Beats Antique), William Close, and the Grammy Award-nominated vocalist Chaytanya. His Ascend album
reached #7 on the iTunes World Music chart and he had two #1 remixes on the Hype Machine. He's traded remixes with
CloZee, Desert Dwellers, iLL Gates, Beats Antique, and SOOHAN.
David's signature sound has led him to work with well-known companies such as Lucas films, Samsung, Sprint, Beats
Audio, Google, Siemens, Nokia, HP, Symantec, Virgin Galactic, Roland, Digg, MacWorld, Best Buy, Flaunt Magazine,
CAA, Red Bull, Warner Music, Atlantic Music, Industrial Light and Magic, Chobani Yogurt, Hotel W, City of San Francisco,
Subpac, TRIPP VR, and many others. He's performed around the world in exotic places such as the pyramids of Giza in
Egypt, India, Thailand, Norway, Canada, Costa Rica, Mexico, Australia, and most major cities in Europe.
David has released 7 albums and 13 EPs in the last 15 years as well as various singles and remixes. David’s songs are
featured on several TV, cable, movies, and video games. Some highlights include the movie Sold (Emma Thompson as
exec producer), Lie to Me (FOX), Outsourced (NBC), Live with Kelly Ripa (ABC), ESPN2, Fox Sports, MTV, MTV2, Fuse,
Showtime, HBO, EA sports, and a Right Guard commercial. His songs are featured on several music compilations
including Sony, Lonely Planet, Red Bull, Craig’s List, and his song “Shout it Out” was in regular rotation at Starbucks.
David has been producing music for meditation and psychedelic assisted therapy for many years. He's the music
facilitator for high profile meditation retreats with the likes of Deepak Chopra, Robert Thurman, Richard Schwartz (IFS),
Sunny Strasburg, Gita Vaid, and others. David worked with Phil Wolfson (MAPS researcher, The Ketamine Papers) on the
K Dome virtual Burning Man Project. He helped to develop the TRIPP meditation phone app plus contributed music and
was the main curator of the music content including well known names such as Moby, Ram Dass, Alan Watts, Desert
Dwellers, Dirtwire and others.. In 2023, David released a highly acclaimed album with meditations by Ram Dass. He's
also the head of music and sound at TRIPP VR, the #1 meditation platform in virtual reality. In 2020, David co-founded
PsyAssist with Sunny Strasburg, a VR and Phone app that features music and guided meditation for psychedelic therapy.
David started his musical journey playing guitar at 8, then playing in the jazz band and marching band in high school. In
his 20’s he was signed to various bands and eventually fell in love with the fusion of world music with EDM. He comes
from a musical family in New Orleans. His grandfather Oscar Rouzan, was a well known jazz musician and his cousin,
Wanda Rouzan, known as the “The sweetheart of New Orleans music”.
