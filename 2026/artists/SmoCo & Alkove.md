SmoCo & Alkove is a live electronic collaboration fusing heavy bass music with improvised instrumental performance. Built around SmoCo’s deep, high-energy dubstep selections and Alkove’s atmospheric live guitar work, the duo creates immersive sets shaped entirely in real time through looping, live effects, and reactive improvisation.

Drawing influence from underground bass culture, cinematic sound design, post-rock textures, and experimental live performance, SmoCo & Alkove blur the line between DJ set and live act. Crushing low end, emotional melodies, ambient layering, and spontaneous moments of chaos and beauty collide to create a performance that is both intimate and explosive.

With roots in Calgary’s creative and underground music community, the project is driven by connection, energy, and the idea that no two sets should ever sound the same.
