multidisciplinary artist jakob salix, is best known for layering deep and melancholy soundscapes, exploring avant garde, ambient folk, downtempo, and dubstep. Mixing only vinyl, the listener is drawn into a world of introspection, diversity, and the weight of our societal fabric; dark and heavy, yet beautiful and gentle - Ready to be deconstructed and reassembled.
