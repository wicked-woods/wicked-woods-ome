Sound Shed is the project of Nicolas Hesson — a Kimberley, BC–based DJ, promoter, and Twitch livestreamer who has quietly become one of the most consistent forces in the Kootenay electronic music scene. 

Why Sound Shed?  At the start of it all, there was a shed in the forest with nothing in it but junk. Nick and his close friend Alpha, converted this Shed into a DJ Room, and named the room The Sound Shed. As time went on, unfortunately Alpha passed on far too early, and that was when Nick adopted the SoundShed alias in his memory — and that ethos of two friends in a small room making something out of nothing still defines the project. To this day, the weekly Twitch stream runs out of that same shed, on an extension cord, to a community across the globe where he greets with the same line every night: Hi, we love you!

As a promoter, SoundShed has built a monthly EDM series from a single night at Bohemian Spirits into a recurring rite at the Kimberley Curling Club, with themed nights like Floral Frequencies, Cupids After Party, and Shamrock Sessions, Fire In The Sky, and many more. Touring guests have included Kytami & Phonik Ops, and West Coast bass legend Abstrakt Sonance.

On the decks, SoundShed is deliberately genre-fluid — house, garage, dubstep, drum & bass — built around the belief that there is too much good music to ever box it in.
