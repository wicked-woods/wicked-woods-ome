Emerencz is a DJ, producer, and head of OBSKUR MUSIC, based in Nelson, British Columbia. Their work as a DJ began in 2009 in southwestern Alberta.

The pioneering house and techno scenes of Chicago, New York, and Detroit first drew them into dance music, laying the foundation for their sound. The early 2000s evolution of tech house gave those influences a deeper, more restrained shape. Stripped-down rhythms meet bubbling, acid-tinged textures and deep synth stabs, creating a hypnotic, late-night atmosphere where warmth, movement, and subtle tension guide the journey. Their music reflects the energy and innovation of electronic music’s earliest days, reimagined for today’s dance floors.
