Based in Kimberley, Oldman has spent the last 14 years cultivating a sound steeped in deep bass pressure, dubbed-out textures, and heavyweight dubwise selections. From the early days of Sleazy Listening events to regular appearances at Wicked Woods, his sets move fluidly through dubstep, dub, and underground bass culture, layered with subtle jazz influences and a strong focus on atmosphere and groove.
