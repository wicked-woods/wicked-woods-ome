Tracy Mihajic (Mihajic Touch) – Host | Fashion Curator | DJ
Fashion and music have always been at the heart of Tracy Mihajic's creative journey. After attending fashion school in Victoria in 2008, she spent years designing clothing, developing a passion for self-expression, individuality, and the transformative power of personal style. In 2018, she brought that passion to her hometown of Golden, BC, by opening Worthy Boutique, a curated consignment store celebrating sustainable fashion, quality garments, and timeless style.
For Tracy, fashion and music have always gone hand in hand. Both create identity, tell stories, and bring communities together. Whether she's curating a runway collection or crafting a dance floor, her goal is the same: to inspire confidence, connection, and creativity.

This year's Wicked Woods Fashion Show brings together Tracy's two lifelong passions. As host and curator, she has assembled a showcase that celebrates creativity, sustainability, and the incredible talent within the festival community. More than a runway show, it's a celebration of self-expression—where fashion meets music, art meets movement, and every outfit tells a story.
