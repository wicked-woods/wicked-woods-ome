Tammra, aka DJ sprouT, has been moving dance floors for over two decades. She brings a finely tuned ear for storytelling through sound, crafting sets that are immersive, full of heart, and just the right amount of sass. As a DJ, event curator, and promoter, her love of music and mix creation has taken her to various corners of the globe, leading her to play at numerous events, nightclubs, and festivals, including Wicked Woods Music Festival, Shambhala Music Festival, Bass Coast Project, and many more.

The profound connection between music and the audience fuels her creativity and inspires her to continually push the boundaries of her craft. sprouT fuses deep, funky house and breaks with bass-forward, melodic, and percussive elements, creating eclectic mixes that flow with intention. She layers juicy sound journeys that ignite the heart and stir the soul.
