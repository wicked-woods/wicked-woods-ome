Tone.D is a Calgary-based DJ known for weaving soulful 140, dubstep, grime, and jazz-influenced bass music into immersive, subsonic journeys. Active in the local underground scene since his teenage years, he has grown from passionate dance floor regular to dedicated selector and community contributor.

Since the project’s inception in 2017, Tone.D has continued to refine a sound grounded in sound system culture, deep low-end pressure, melodic atmosphere, and carefully chosen rhythms. His sets balance weight and warmth, moving between meditative dubstep, percussive grooves, and uplifting moments that bring the dance floor together.

With an ear for emotional depth and a love for underground culture, Tone.D delivers sets that feel intentional, soulful, and deeply connected to the dance floor.
