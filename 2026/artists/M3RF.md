Surrounded by music, his father playing guitar, harmonica, and button accordion, M3RF born into a musical family in Newfoundland. He  started out his  musical career in an award winning boys choir and grew playing classical piano and trumpet.  Before he was a teen he had a hi- fi stereo and full discographies of Zeppelin, Floyd, Queen and Yes etc. Then in the 80’s and nineties fell I love with hip-hop, punk, metal, techno and house. He draws inspiration rom Beethoven to Maestro Fresh Wes, Daft Punk and The Chemical brothers to Bad Religion and NOFX. 

Soulful, heartfelt music from every conceivable genre and their infinite subgenera’s (‘cept bro-step) created  the underlying conditions that inspired this, Innocent gospel, loving Newfoundlander to evolve into a bonafide, British Columbian rave lord. 

20+ Years living in the Kootenay’s working and playing at Shambhala, only added further nourishment to his passion for House and DNB.  M3RF is impossible to pigeon hole, and he is comfortable playing at anytime for any room.  Whether he’s  Lubing up the dance floor with Garage House for A.Skillz or Orchestrating a sexy, Down-tempo bass vibe on a Sunset beach in Thailand.  Having an intimate Jam with friends at Cantina del Centro or throwing down one of his legendary 4+ hour closers at Wicked Woods, this Fractal Forest Alumni, is guaranteed satisfy even the most experienced pallet with the familiar and unexpected.
