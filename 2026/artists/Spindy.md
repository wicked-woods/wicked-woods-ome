Spindy was submersed in music
at an early age, sounds both old
and new resonated from her dad’s
record shops. Noted as a Kootenay
Drum and Bass Resident, Spindy
has performed at festivals and
events across Western Canada.
She has shared the decks with
artists such as, Alix Perez, DJ SS
Grooverider, Aphrodite and more.

Spindy’s sets are laced with con-
trast, exhibiting both dark and light
realms. Known for her attention to
detail and style, Spindy’s unique
taste creates a playful yet cerebral
dancefloor experience - uniting
ravers of all flavours.
