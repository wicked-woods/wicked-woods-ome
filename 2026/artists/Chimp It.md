Blending the richness of cutting edge electronic music with the depth and emotion of
blistering guitar licks and live vocals

Chimp It’s signature move is to keep their fans guessing, as they weave their original
material into edits and remixes from their favourite artists and peers, moving through 140,
UKG, Dubstep, DnB or whatever fresh gems they’ve uncovered in the far reaches of the
music-verse.

Alongside releasing music on Gravitas and DoYu, Chimp It have been selected for prime
time slots at events such as Shambhala, Wicked Woods, Future Forest, Arc’teryx Academy & more. 
They have headlined many club/bar shows as well as landing direct
support slots and jamming live with acts such as Moontricks, Dirtwire, Lazy Syrup Orchestra, The Librarian, Burchill, Pineo & Loeb and Kytami & Phonik
Ops.
