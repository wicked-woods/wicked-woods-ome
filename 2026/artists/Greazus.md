GREAZUS is the creative marriage of Vancouver’s HxdB & Patrik Cure. Now a decade on, their project remains a beacon for fun, forward-thinking, hybrid dance music. They’ve managed to stay ahead of the trends, creating their own unique niche by side-stepping genre limitations. 

They traverse a large breadth of sounds in their discography, adeptly making beats in a variety of styles. They bridging the sounds of Club, UK Garage, Electro, Breaks, UK Hardcore, Jungle, Footwork, Grime and more. Re-imagining sounds from the past into a modern framework, their sound is familiar, yet entirely their own.

They’ve released on several top underground record labels and are in-demand remixers and DJs, and have had their music supported by countless top DJs. Their sets are always memorable, high energy affairs, which has earned them a reputation as large-draw favorites at Bass Coast and Shambhala Music Festivals and several notable international festival appearances.
