Polymath and global DJ, ENiGMA Dubz is fast becoming one of the most influential producers in bass music - flexing from punishing drops to cinematic electronic soundscapes across multiple genres.

His series of mixtapes have made significant impact with Mixtape 1: #Heaters, featuring seven collaborations with some of the hottest acts in the global dubstep scene - including PAV4N, Bommer, Snowy and MYTHM - followed by Mixtape 2: #Vibes showcasing the soulful future garage, breaks and melodic bass side of the ENiGMA Dubz sound - including collaborations with leading electronic vocalists Lauren L'aimant and Leo Wood.

He has seen support from leading tastemakers including UKF, Kiss FM & Radio 1Xtra, alongside spins from dons DJ Q, Caspa & London Elektricity - as well as regularly topping the Beatport charts and landing Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music playlists across dubstep, UKG, drum & bass, bassline, grime and electronica.

The producer made his debut release on CRUCAST with the viral UKG Window Kid collab, Playing Out - and was handpicked by Mike Skinner to feature on The Street’s legendary fabric mixtape.  

On the live side, ENiGMA Dubz has toured across Europe, North America and Australasia - including playing some of the biggest bass music festivals in the world, such as Excision's Lost Lands, Rampage, Infrasound & Sound Haven - alongside regular appearances with DDD founders, Truth. 

For ENiGMA Dubz, the Awakening chapter is just beginning. 2026 will be the biggest yet.
