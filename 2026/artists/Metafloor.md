Metafloor is a key figure in Western Canada’s underground bass music scene, known for genre-fluid blends of bass-driven club music. He has released music on labels including Artikal Music UK, Infernal Sounds, Defrostatica, Aufect Platinum, and Deep Dark & Dangerous, and collaborated with artists such as Fixate, Mat The Alien, Doctor Jeep, Coltcuts and Hypho. In 2011, he co-founded Calgary bass collective Sub Chakra, and in 2026, launched Support System - a highly curated, hypnotic rave experience. Whether in the studio or behind the decks, Metafloor explores sound with curiosity, intensity, and a refusal to stay in one lane.
