Dolla Hilz

DJ | Community Builder | Curator of Creative Spaces

Bass-loving DJ blending hip hop, dancehall & global bass into high-energy, soul-driven sets. Community builder, curator & dancefloor igniter. ????

Dolla Hilz is a Revelstoke, BC–based DJ, dancer, and event producer known for high-energy, genre-blending sets rooted in bass and sound system culture. A lifelong music lover, she got hooked by Calgary’s underground scene and has travelled internationally to experience reggae, hip hop, and global bass communities. On the decks, she delivers seamless journeys through hip hop, grime, dancehall, dub, afrobeats, jungle, and house, bringing soulful, body-moving energy and magnetic presence to every performance. 

She has played across Western Canada and internationally, contributed mixes to SWERVE, SubChakra, Chill Bangers, and more, co-founded Flatmates Production, and created large-scale interactive installations for Revelstoke’s LUNA Art Festival. Committed to community, Hilz founded a BIPOC Youth Dance Scholarship, leads an accessible mobility program for seniors, and co-facilitates no-cost grief groups through the Revelstoke Hospice Society. She established her private practice, Wholehearted Counselling in 2025. Her artistry is guided by intention, visibility, and connection, bringing joy and care to every dancefloor, workshop, and community space she touches.
