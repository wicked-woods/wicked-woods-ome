Born in Manchester, UK and now based in Perth, Australia, KALI is a producer, DJ, and founder of ILLICIT — a record label platforming some of the most exciting emerging talent from around the world.

His own music has found a home on some of the world's most coveted platforms and labels — UKF, Deep Dark & Dangerous, Duploc, White Peach Records, and Youngsta's legendary Sentry Records — a catalogue that speaks for itself.

Now returning to Canada for his second appearance at Wicked Woods Festival, KALI is no stranger to BC. Since his debut tour in 2023, he's become a genuine fixture across the province, earning his place on stages through the music rather than the hype. And this time, you might just see a few ILLICIT artists make a surprise appearance during his set. Wicked Woods is easily in my top 3 Canadian festivals.
