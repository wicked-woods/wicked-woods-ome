Garneau is a Vancouver-based DJ, producer and head of electro imprint Club Designs, known for percussive, stripped-back club tracks that fuse house, techno, electro and ghetto house. With releases on Magic City, Chapter 3, Philthtrax and Posse Up! — plus collaborations with Chicago legends like DJ Slugo, Parris Mitchell and the late DJ Deeon — he’s become a cornerstone of the western Canadian underground and a regular at Bass Coast, including a Boiler Room x Bass Coast appearance and his breakbeat-driven side project Energy Boyz with Destrata.
