Jaq Taforo plays piano and his solo set dives deep into a vast realm of genres using a keyboard and looping pedal to layer synths and beats, steeped in jazz adjacent harmonies and soulful melodies. Originally from from Mt Shasta California, Jaq made Nelson his home and has been regularly performing throughout the kootenays in an array of different groups and styles. He often improvises beyond playing his original compositions making each set a unique journey through sound keeping the audience on their toes for what new groove will drop next.
