Born into the electronic music scene in Kelowna, BC, Smashley discovered her passion for DJing early and began honing her craft in 2011. What started as a love for the dance floor quickly evolved into a dedication to moving crowds and creating unforgettable moments through sound.

Now based in Prince George, Smashley has become a familiar name across the BC Interior and Northern scene, playing everything from underground parties to large-scale festivals. Her festival credits include Electric Love, ValhallaFest, Back to the Forest, The Groove, Sexy Sounds, and beyond.

Whether she’s playing an intimate room or a packed festival stage, Smashley brings energy, intention, and a deep respect for the culture to every set.
