The sound of Eskapist seamlessly blends depth of sound design, enchanting melodies, and lush atmospheres. His live sets are crafted to bring the crowd on a sonic journey of low-end intensity, ethereal space, and beauty in darkness. The Eskapist sound will bring out both bass faces and states of pure euphoria on the same dance floor. With past festival appearances from coast to coast, this upcoming Canadian artist is on a mission to bring his unique sound wherever it will take him. With his unreleased productions being meticulously crafted constantly, Eskapist is intent on making an impact on the electronic music world.
