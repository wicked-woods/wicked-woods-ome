Vancouver-based coupled duo Average Citizens are known for their wobbly basslines, infectious feel good energy and high impact performances. Blending elements of hip-hop, bass house, and UKG, their sound delivers a high-energy, swagger-driven experience that keeps dance floors locked in from start to finish. Over the past year, they've toured relentlessly, playing over 40 shows across Canada, and the U.S., with standout appearances at Miami Music Week, FVDED in the Park, Foundation Series, and Shambhala.

Their international reach continues to expand, with performances in Spain, Mexico, Taiwan, Germany, England, El Salvador, Ecuador, and Costa Rica. DJ Mag has spotlighted them as rising stars, calling their sound a carefree fusion of hip-hop, future house, and UK-inspired bass, irresistibly danceable. On the production front, Average Citizens have landed multiple charting releases on labels like Garage Shared, 3000 Bass, InStereo, and Jungle Cakes, including a #1 UKG release on Beatport via YosH and a recent Top 10 in Bass House, a rare feat for fully independent artists.

Their tracks have earned support from global heavyweights such as David Guetta, DJ Q, AC Slater, Don Diablo, Richie Hawtin, and Dr. Fresch, with radio play on Diplo’s Revolution, Insomniac Radio and Kiss FM Bristol. With a growing global fanbase and an unmistakable energy that translates both on stage and in the studio, Average Citizens are rapidly carving out their place as one of the most exciting forces in today’s underground scene, and this is just the beginning.
