JeniMack is a music curator and House DJ based in British Columbia,
Canada. Raised in the '70s by hippies - she grew up in BC's underground rave
scene in the '90's, an environment known for its focus on electronic music,
visual arts, self expression, and community building.
Expanding from her origins on Vancouver Island, JeniMack is actively involved
in events and festivals especially on the the Island and the Kootenays. She
contributes to these events by designing and constructing stages and
experiential spaces and is particularly recognized for organizing and hosting
memorable gatherings, often in remote settings such as forests and beaches.
With a long-standing passion for electronic dance music (EDM) dating back to
the early 1990s, JeniMack began to focus seriously on mixing in 2019. Since
then, she has performed at various small festivals and events, continuously
developing her distinct sound. Her musical palette is significantly shaped by
decades of listening to genres such as blues, reggae, hard rock, hip hop and
grunge. Her style is most accurately categorized as Indie Dance and Bass
House, often featuring subtle yet impactful undercurrents of Latin, Funky, and
Afro House influences.
