E-LeV-8 (Philthy City DNB/Wicked Woods)

E-LeV-8 has been a heavyweight staple in the DnB scene in Calgary for over a decade, representing Philthy City, and having opened for massive overseas and local artists like T>I, Saxxon, Serial Killas, and Critical Impact, and also performing some of the most illustrious festivals such as Wicked Woods, Fozzy Fest, The Womp, LEMF, Emotion (SK), as well as hosting regular nights throughout Calgary vast repertoire of electronic nights in the club and more, he is no stranger to making dance floors packed and astounding his audience with flawless mixing and sound design through his own productions. If you like a smoothe blend of Jump-Up, Neurofunk, and Jungle, then get ready to be E-LeV-8ed!!
