PattyMuss is a dynamic LGBTQ+ DJ from the East Kootenays, bringing infectious beats and unapologetic energy to the dance floor. But his journey into the booth started long before the decks, it began in the crowd. With over a decade of festival experience, PattyMuss has lived the magic from the rail to the dust, and after years of mixing music in his head day after day, he finally decided to step into the DJ life.

Now blending his love for UK speed garage, bass house, and drum & bass with his signature house, disco, and pop remixes (always with a queer twist), PattyMuss delivers sets that move you from your head straight to your feet. Expect heavy bass, cheeky garage swings, and rhythms that refuse to let you stand still.

PattyMuss doesn’t just play music, he creates spaces where you can dance like nobody’s judging. And here? They’re not.
