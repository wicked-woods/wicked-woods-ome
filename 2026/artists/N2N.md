N2N has quietly emerged as one of dance music's most compelling underground forces. Unpredictable and emotionally unfiltered, his performances push past celebration into catharsis. Drawing from disco, house, funk, and hip hop, his sound is funky, bouncy, and intentionally hard to pin down.
A fearless mixer with an instinct for tension and release, N2N has ignited dance floors everywhere from intimate clubs to major festival stages, with standout appearances at Desert Hearts Festival, Elements, and Shambhala. His presence is commanding without ego-setting the tone and daring people to meet it. Fresh off 70+ shows in 2025 and backed by industry tastemakers including Toolroom, Repopulate Mars, and GDB, the momentum is building.
2026 sees N2N bring his imprint Dance Therapy to the forefront—a philosophy rooted in self-expression, release, and the belief in the healing power of the dance floor. When he's not behind the decks, he's tending to an ever-growing plant collection or whispering sweet nothings to his cats: Bucket, Shoe, Tofu, and Jasmine.
