Kimati is a bass music producer and DJ known for his heavy, futuristic sound design and infectious energy. With releases on labels like Wubaholics, Morflo, WaveCraftCollective, and Doyu Digital, he's established himself as a respected name in the bass music community.
Born in Newfoundland, Kimati's passion for electronic music led him to start producing his own tracks, blending dark basslines with futuristic textures and rhythmic patterns that defy gravity. His music gets bodies moving and minds expanding.
When he's not in the studio, Kimati tears up the decks at clubs and festivals around the world. His high-energy live performances are a testament to his dedication to his craft.
