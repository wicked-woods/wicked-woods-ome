Sully, the nickname and moniker of Detroit-based producer Christopher Sullivant, has quickly become synonymous with cutting-edge design, dynamic production, and high-voltage performances.

Since breaking onto the scene in 2019, his distinct identity and unmistakable sonic DNA have embedded themselves into the evolving landscape of modern bass music. His earliest music acted as a catalyst, earning widespread acclaim from listeners, tastemakers, and industry veterans alike. Sully built on his established success with a string of powerful releases on respected footprints among the likes of WAKAAN, Deadbeats, Dim Mak, and Kannibalen.

The 2023 release of his debut LP and subsequent headline tour marked a defining chapter in his artistry. Since then, Sully’s catalog has only continued to expand with heavy-hitting collaborative records alongside Peekaboo, Liquid Stranger, Mersiv, and NGHTMRE and Flowdan, reinforcing his presence among the genre’s elite while maintaining an uncompromising commitment to creative risk.

Seamlessly fusing elements of gritty dubstep, trap, drum & bass, hip-hop, and more, this duality has made Sully a fixture on revered stages, including performances at Bass Canyon, Lost Lands, and WAKAAN Music Festival, along with iconic venues like WaMu Theater and Red Rocks Amphitheatre.

Leaving a chrome-drenched mark on everything he touches, Sully isn’t just shaping the future of bass music — he’s leaving an imprint on its very foundation.
