Dorothy is a DJ and Producer rooted in
the Drum and Bass scene of
Western Canada. His sound carries a
liquid energy — bass-heavy and powerful,
without losing the flow. Known for tailoring
his sets to the stage and moment, he brings
both versatility and authenticity to the decks,
delivering performances that adapt to the vibe
while staying true to his sound.
