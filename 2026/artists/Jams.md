JAMS [403 DnB / DnB Girls / CGAgency] 
JAMS’ influence extends beyond the DJ booth, as co-owner of the revered DnB Girls Collective (championing female artists globally), she is a true community leader. Her passion burns beneath these labels; Jungle, DnB, and Hip-Hop fuel her fire as she explores every corner of bass music, guaranteed to ignite any dance floor.
Jams' talent is undeniable. Back in 2009, she went from beginner to competition podium in just six months, placing 3rd out of 40 DJs. She quickly became a top DJ & Event Promoter in Edmonton before migrating east to Montreal. In 2014, she landed in Calgary with the legendary 403DnB crew, building connections and community along the way.
Jams has curated a decade of digital DnB content, reaching an impressive milestone of hosting 100+ guest mixes through ‘The DnB Girls Podcast’, a platform launching rising artists into the spotlight.
Jams' signature DJ sets have been featured and performed live at renowned international festivals like Envision in Costa Rica, as well as Canadian staples such as Shambhala, MEME, Evolve, Motion Notion, Wicked Woods, and numerous coast-to-coast tours, gaining widespread recognition across the country. Her roots continue to run deep in the Canadian Drum & Bass scene.
