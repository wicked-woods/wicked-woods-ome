Grim Sickers: The Unstoppable Voice of UK Rap

Over the past 15 years, Grim Sickers — real name Paul Dowman — has evolved into one of the most respected and authentic voices in British rap. Hailing from Swindon, his journey from underground beginnings to becoming a genre-spanning artist has been powered by consistency, individuality, and undeniable presence.

With Grime staples like “Kane” and “Open The Till” etched into UK music history, Sickers has long proven himself as a commanding force at 140 BPM. His influence and versatility have led to collaborations with some of the biggest names in the scene, including JME, Ghetts, Scorcher, Mike Skinner, and The Manor — consistently proving his ability to hold his own among UK rap royalty.

In 2025, he joined Ocean Wisdom on a nationwide tour, further cementing his reputation as a magnetic live performer capable of igniting crowds across the country.

Beyond the stage, Sickers is a breakout personality in the digital space, with a viral TikTok presence that has earned him millions of views. His larger-than-life character, raw humour, and unfiltered honesty have made him one of the most engaging UK artists online.

In a bold move into gaming, he recently voiced Oscar the Tiger in Fortnite, adding yet another string to his creative bow and underlining his ever-expanding cultural reach.

Whether tearing down a set, building community online, Grim Sickers remains a one-of-one — a true original whose story is far from finished.
