Bass Cat is a Kootenay-based DJ known for versatile, crowd-driven sets and a sound that blends bass-heavy energy with laid-back mountain vibes. Moving between drum and bass, jungle, house, chill beats, reggaeton, hip hop, country remixes, and even glam metal, she’s built a following of listeners spanning the globe. She’s played festivals and events including Wicked Woods, Revel, 420 Fest, Labour of Love and Lifted, and has appeared on Radio Free Winlove and Kootenay Co-op Radio. Her sets have taken her everywhere from clubs like Bloom Nightclub in Nelson and the Commodore in Kamloops to restaurants, yoga studios, beaches, house parties and school dances. DJ Bass Cat keeps things light—playing what feels good and building sets that keep people moving.
