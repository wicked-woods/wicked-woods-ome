Karnage - Nagoya, Japan

A Japanese artist based in Nagoya.
Karnage produces music characterized by delicate sound processing rooted in bass music, combined with inorganic and abstract sound design reminiscent of noise, metal, and dub. Blending a decadent, industrial aesthetic with forward-thinking beats, he creates a unique form of experimental music. In addition to producing original tracks, he is active as a DJ, performs live sets, and contributes music to various projects.

He began producing music in 2011, focusing primarily on dubstep. While living in Detroit at the time, he was also active as a DJ. In Detroit, he became a member of the legendary crew DatsWatsUp! and gained experience organizing numerous parties. Karnage has toured extensively across the U.S. and Europe. In 2019, he performed at Outlook Festival in Croatia and Star Festival in Japan. In 2025, he performed at Infrasound Equinox in America.

He has released a number of works to date. In 2020, his collaborative album Facing Inner Struggle with UK MC Rider Shafique garnered praise for its dynamic chemistry between vocalist and producer and its wide-ranging sound design, all built within the framework of dubstep.

In 2024, he released Dystopian Synthesis, a work that expresses a dystopian aesthetic built around the core setup of his live performance gear. The release further solidified his distinctive sound and artistic identity.

In 2025, Karnage released his debut album Moments of Fleeting Existence on Daymare Recordings. The album is themed around Detroit, where he spent his formative years. It reflects the industrial landscape he personally experienced, intertwined with human emotions shaped by migration and the COVID-19 pandemic, expressed through collaborations with various artists.

Recently, Karnage has returned his focus to producing dubstep, the genre that lies at the core of his roots. His increasingly refined sound is poised to bring a new wave to the dubstep scene.
