Mt. Doyle is one of Vancouver Island’s most versatile DJs, known for genre-blending sets that move seamlessly between Drum & Bass, House, Funk, Hip-Hop and Bass music, all delivered with a soulful West Coast touch. Originally emerging from the Drum & Bass scene, pizza in hand, Doyle quickly built a reputation across British Columbia for his musical range and carefully curated mixes. Over the past decade he has performed at clubs, lounges and major festivals throughout the Pacific Northwest, including Shambhala, Lightning In A Bottle, Rifflandia, Electric Love, Wicked Woods, Astral Harvest, Bamboo Bass, Friendzy Fest and Tall Tree Festival. Along the way he has shared the stage with a wide range of international artists including Aphrodite, Camo & Krooked, Ed Solo, A.Skillz, Stickybuds, The Funk Hunters, Chali 2na, JFB, Dub FX, Rusko, Technimatic, LSB & DRS, Serial Killaz, Dub Pistols, Ray Keith, Netsky and Andy C. In 2016 Doyle launched The Hydration Mix Series, a monthly Liquid Drum & Bass podcast alongside Akela, Willisist, Erski, Reflektor, Lasu and DJ Surgeon. That same year he expanded into live performance as a vocalist with Victoria hip-hop band Illvis Freshly, bringing a new dimension to his musical output. Whether delivering afternoon delights, deep sunrise grooves, or genre-bending dance parties, Mt. Doyle continues to keep crowds moving while pushing beyond stylistic boundaries.
