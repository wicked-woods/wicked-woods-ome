Shallow Wells (Nate O’Hearn & Darian Troy) operate in the tension between genres—where poetry fractures into post-hiphop, collides with rave energy, and bleeds raw emotion through meticulous craft. Formed after a chance cypher meeting in 2014, the Kimberley, BC duo built their sound from the ground up, self-releasing This is a Sinking Ship (2017), reimagining it with Kootenay electronic producers in 2019, and later shaping their sophomore project Kept The Crown (2022) from clips created at fire lookout towers and closet recording booths while working remote Albertan wilderness posts.
Their evolution from DIY isolation to deliberate artistry has sharpened both their production and their live presence, culminating in a breakout performance at Wicked Woods Festival, alongside shows opening for Snak The Ripper and community-driven fundraiser events.
With radio support for their 2024 single Lullaby and a growing network spanning sound engineers, poets, and underground promoters, the duo continue to carve out a space that feels both cerebral and chaoticpeace.
Based in Kimberley, BC, Shallow Wells are pushing further into that unknown—refining their sound and expanding their catalog, most recently with the EP Don’t Let Me Gho.
