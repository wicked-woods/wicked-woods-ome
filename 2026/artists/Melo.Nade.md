The name Melo.Nade stems from the fluid flow of the sound. Born and raised in Calgary and living in the Kootenays, Melo.Nade is a classically trained pianist turned sonic architect - weaving journeys felt in the body as much in the soul.

With nearly 20 years behind the decks and 14 of those on festival stages, Melo.Nade has honed a rare ability to read a crowd and take them places they didn't know they needed to go. Balancing dark and light, expect deep dubstep, lustful world beats, breaks, grime, hip-hop, trip-hop, soul/R&B, and thought-provoking bass music – you know… Heavy Chill. 

His sets blur the line between DJ and storyteller. These are the creative expressions of an untethered soul. Music is medicine.
