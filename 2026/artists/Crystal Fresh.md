Crystal Fresh

(DnB Girls, Stars33d Syndicate, Village Stage)
DnB/Halftime/House/Breaks/Bass/Hiphop


Armed with a background in natural healing techniques coupled with an addiction to high energy double-drops and heavy bass music, Crystal Fresh transforms dance floors world-wide into healing vortexes with her passionate style and grimy beats.

As an internationally touring DJ in search of the ultimate party since 2002, she has acquired culturally diverse experience in event promotion, music production, live performance, MC/Hosting, and top-notch mixing skills. This enthusiastic and successful West Coast Canadian Female has established and mastered an enterprising style of mixing that delves deep into the healing culture of bass music.  

As well as running a Music and Wellness charity for At-Risk Youth, she is a resident DJ at Shambhala Music Festival's Village Stage and annually hosts the DnB Girls Takeover. With her healing vibes in full force and her fresh, energetic beats pushing her passion, this Goddess of Bass is making big waves!
