SHORT BIO:
Mraki is a DJ shaped by the Kootenay rave and underground dance music
scene, bringing a movement focused approach to the dancefloor. Their
sets are intentional and versatile, pulling from a wide range of
sounds beyond any single genre while staying grounded in global club
sounds and percussion led music. Built for the space they are played
in, Mraki moves fluidly across tempos and time feels, centering
momentum, connection, and collective energy. It always comes back to
those moments when the room locks in and we are all in the dance
together.

FULL BIO:
Born and raised in the Kootenays, Mraki is a DJ and community builder
shaped by the local rave and underground dance music scene. Those
early spaces laid the groundwork for what they create now, shaping
how they approach sound, movement, and the dancefloor.
While global club sounds and percussion led music are central to
their sets, particularly in club and festival environments, Mraki
also pulls from a wide range of sounds beyond any single genre. Each
set is shaped with intention and built to suit its moment. They often
work across tempos and time feels, letting movement rather than BPM
guide the dancefloor. It always comes back to those moments when the
room locks in and we are all in the dance together.
Beyond the decks, Mraki leads the vision and curation behind Global
Heat, creating platforms that prioritize care & context. Their work
centers artists & sounds that shape diversity, with an emphasis on
building intentional spaces & community around them.
