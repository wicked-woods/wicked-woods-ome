Originally from Calgary, but now based out of Vernon, British Columbia. MoJo has had a love for the electronic music scene for many years, and captivates her exposure to bass music culture as her inspiration towards her energetic sets. With seamless mixing she likes to tell a story with her sets, expelling her love and passion within the scene, making an impact on others with her energy. While mostly being passionate about low-end and experimental bass, she doesn't shy away from pushing boundaries and has opened for artists such as Atyya, Stylust, Woofax, and Burchill. Don't be fooled by her small and innocent outward form, as she is known to throw down the nastiest of beats.
