Percusshunkidd began her journey behind the decks in Ottawa, Ontario, after years of being deeply immersed in the club scene as both a promoter and passionate music lover. It was there she fell in love with the pulse of House music. After relocating to the West Coast four years ago, she quickly embedded herself in the local scene, continuing to evolve her sound and presence.
Known for blending Tech House and House rhythms with an infectious energy, Percusshunkidd brought her unique style to stages at Electric Love Music Festival
2024 and Victoria Pride Festival in the Park 2023. In 2025, she has played Ignite the Arts Music Festival, Asundance Festival, Electric Love Music Festlva and Wicked Woods Music Festival; continuing her presence in the West Coast music scene.
For Percusshunkidd, music is more than performance - its connection. “Being part of a community of like-minded human beings has been my ultimate goal,” she says. “My West Coast adventure has been that, and so much more.”
