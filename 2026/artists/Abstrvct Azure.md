Lynn is a Two-Spirited artist who is recognized for bringing vibrations of love, support, and authenticity to the community. With a background as a visual artist, graphic designer, and print specialist—now she’s expanding into her other passions of DJing and music production with deep roots in the 90’s drum & bass and early 2000’s dubstep, she brings heavy drops and iconic sounds to her sets.
