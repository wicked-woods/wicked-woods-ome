Miss Fudge (DnB Girls / Big Kitty Crew)

Miss Fudge brings fire with her soulful lyrics and haunting vocals, blending deep basslines with classic amen breaks. With collaborations leading to releases on labels across Canada and the UK—including Machinist Music, Midnight Sun Recordings, Black Tuna Recordings, DnB Girls, Natty Dub Recordings, and Dutty Bass—she continues her steady rise in the bass music scene.
