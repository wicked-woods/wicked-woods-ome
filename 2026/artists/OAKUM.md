OAKUM is the electronic project of Vancouver-based producer and visual artist Kieran Jenkins. His music moves between quiet atmosphere, emotional melody, and cinematic bass, shaped by forest textures, detailed production, and a visual world built around nature, contrast, and release.

On stage, OAKUM brings that world into a live AV set with custom synced visuals, performance-driven transitions, and music that moves from intimate moments into heavy, high-energy release. His recent work includes the 2026 project UMBRA and the beginning of TERRA, an elemental album cycle exploring water, air, earth, fire, and cosmos.

OAKUM has performed at Shambhala Music Festival, Friendzy Fest, Red Room Vancouver, Traverse Nightclub, Globe Hall, and Putnam Place, with a sound that sits somewhere between cinematic storytelling, melodic bass, and the weight of a festival set.
