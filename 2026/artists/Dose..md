Dose. is a multifaceted DJ duo consisting of partners Max & Taylor. Their musical style flows through genre bending Techno that fuses sounds from the punk, disco, & trance eras...into what they call, Sluthouse. In their hometown, Dose. also curates underground events + their annual Re-Union Music Festival, performs weekly on Kootenay Coop Radio, & manages the bookings for their company Dose.Productions. Having found solace in the international House & Techno scene, Dose. pushes for the same inclusivity & equality within their own musical sphere; they consistently strive to represent fem folk, women, queer, & everyone in-between. It is their strong mandate to always have gender balance & equal representation within their music & event curation. 
Music is their medium & equality is their goal.
