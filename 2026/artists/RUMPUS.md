Award-winning DJ and producer RUMPUS is pushing the boundaries of house
music, seamlessly blending his productions with live drumming to create a
unique and electrifying experience for his fans. His high-energy performances,
influenced by his roots as a punk rock drummer, have earned him the moniker
RUMPUS—meaning a noisy uproar; a commotion.
