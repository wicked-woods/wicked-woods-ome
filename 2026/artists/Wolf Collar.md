Wolf Collar is an Indigenous bass DJ / Producer from Siksika Nation, blending deep 140 baselines with native - inspired vocals and modern electronic energy. Known for his heavy bass-filled sets that merge ceremony and dubstep. Wolf Collar channels the heartbeat of the plains through wubs, growls, and raw dum textures. His sound bridges traditional identity with future-driven production standing as both homage and evolution. A recent performer at Wicked Woods Music Festival 2022/23/24. Wolf Collar continues to carve space for indigenous voices in the electronic scene - a rising force from Treaty 7 territory pushing bass culture with spirit and intention.
