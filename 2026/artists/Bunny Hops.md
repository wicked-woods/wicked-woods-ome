Bunny Hops is a DJ and producer based in Banff, Canada, known for her high-energy, genre-bending sets, versatility, and magnetic presence behind the decks. She crafts each performance with care and intention — from daytime après-ski sessions to peak-time dancefloors.

Her sound weaves together house, disco, funk, UKG, breaks, bass, club, techno, and beyond, always tailored to the time and place. As a producer, her releases have landed in the Top 10 on Beatport for Breaks and been featured on several editorial playlists, including Best New Breaks.
Hops has quickly become a regular on the Western Canadian festival circuit, with appearances at Wicked Woods, Friendzy Fest, The Downtown Defrost, The Womp, HeARTburn, Neon Jungle, and more.

Beyond the booth, Bunny Hops is a driving force in the Bow Valley electronic music scene. Her curated monthly series, With Intention, brings diverse electronic artists to Banff each winter, fostering connections between local, regional, and national scenes. She also runs regular Open Decks events, mentors emerging DJs, and teaches introductory DJ courses aimed at supporting the next generation of artists.

Committed to the craft, the culture, and the dancefloor, Bunny Hops is just getting started.
