Just a couple of birds playing house!

Kootenay based female DJ due Birdhaus - formed by Bella V & Angie Mo are known for their feel good sets, blending different styles of house music and breaks. True tastemakers with an ability to read the room and bring the right vibes to every dance floor. From intimate gatherings in the woods, to the stages like The Living Room and The Pagoda Stage at Shambhala Music Festival - These birds bring the vibes wherever they land.
