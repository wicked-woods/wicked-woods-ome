Trevor Galore - PK Sound 
Alberta, Canada
Trevor Galore - A.K.A. (DJ Twotimer) is a mix of drum and bass and melodic
bass music. One of the original members of the PK Crew from Calgary Alberta,
Trevor has had his hand in most of Western Canada's EDM festival and concert
scene over the last 20 years as a Production Manager and an artist
Trevor started his DJ career in 1998, has been a part of the Village Stage since
2005. Playing some of the biggest stages in Canada along side some of the
globe’s top talent. He is usually responsible for building the nights dancefloor and an all-around positive vibe.
