Naturalist is made up of Mister Moffat (Sam) and FlatSpin (Caleb). The brothers from Fernie have been making waves in the Canadian music scene for over a decade. Playing festivals and clubs from Coast to Coast. Sharing the stage with acts from all over the world.They are known for their feel-good energy, blending genres of funky House and Breakbeat to transform the dancefloor with high vibrations.

NATURALIST: Space Disco & High-Vibration House (Fernie, BC)


Naturalist is the brotherly DJ duo (Mister Moffat & FlatSpin) from Fernie, BC, renowned for their infectious, feel-good
energy and ability to transform any dance floor. They deliver a sound they've dubbed 'Space Disco'—a blend of
high-energy house, classic disco, and breakbeats.
